Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Project Delivered handles information when you use the app.

Information we collect

Project Delivered may collect and store information that you provide or create while using the app, including:

  • Your display name, username and email address.
  • Your account identifier.
  • Messages and other content you send in conversations.
  • Photos, videos, documents and other files you choose to share.
  • Voice messages and other audio content you choose to send.
  • Profile information and app preferences, such as notification and privacy settings.

How we use information

We use this information to provide the core functionality of Project Delivered, including account access, direct and group messaging, media and file sharing, profile management, message history, read receipts, online-status features and account security.

Data storage and service providers

Project Delivered uses Supabase to provide backend services such as authentication, database storage and file storage. Information needed to provide the service may be processed and stored through Supabase infrastructure.

Tracking and advertising

Project Delivered does not use your information for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Your choices

You can manage supported privacy and notification settings from within the app. You can also update your profile information and, where available, delete your Project Delivered account from the Profile section.

Account deletion

If you choose to delete your account, Project Delivered will remove your account access and associated profile information in accordance with the app's deletion process. Some conversation content may remain where necessary to preserve shared conversation history for other participants.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures intended to protect information handled by the app. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

Project Delivered is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please use the contact details provided on the Project Delivered Support page.
